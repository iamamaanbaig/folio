Saved millions of dollars in taxes

By improving stakeholder visibility and coordination across a complex, multi‑stakeholder process, helping prevent the delays that result in tax liability.

Role
Product Designer
Team
Product Manager, Sprint Master, Designer (me), Developers
Client
LaSalle Investment Management
Problem

The problem

Stakeholders around the world managed a complex business process (GIFA), working on overlapping tasks with limited visibility into each other’s decisions and progress.

Data discrepancies

Stakeholders kept separate versions of information, creating inconsistencies that needed time-consuming reconciliation.

Limited visibility

Stakeholders lacked clear updates on task progress and changes made by others, making it harder to coordinate work and spot delays.

Challenges

Project challenges

Challenge How we approached it
1

Requirements given as solutions

We received an initial brief that outlined proposed solutions before the underlying user problems were fully understood.

How we approached it

To uncover the real challenges, we questioned the existing process, the stakeholders involved, their responsibilities and the difficulties they faced.

2

Limited access to users

The stakeholders involved were occupied with other critical organisational matters, making it difficult to schedule time with them.

How we approached it

After learning who was involved and their workflows, and securing their time, we made the most of each conversation by asking focused questions about their workflows, responsibilities and pain points to uncover the underlying problems.

Solutions

Problems we discovered & solutions we designed

Problem 1

Errors and roadblocks went unnoticed

Editors only found errors and roadblocks when they had time to check GIFA.

Solution designed

Clear process view with automated alerts

Every step of a fund’s process in one view, with alerts the moment something needs attention.

Impact
  • Improved process visibility
  • Early issue detection
  • Faster corrective action
Problem 2

Changes and decisions lacked visibility

Stakeholders couldn’t see each other’s overlapping actions, leading to data discrepancies and time-consuming reconciliation.

Solution designed

Activity log

Records every data change and the decision behind it, so everyone is on the same page.

Impact
  • Greater transparency
  • Less reconciliation
  • Better-informed decisions
Problem 3

Approvers manually reviewed hundreds of awards

Approvers combed through hundreds of awards by hand to find the ones that matched their criteria.

Solution designed

Criteria-based award filtering

Predefined criteria automatically flag the awards that match.

Impact
  • Less unnecessary review
  • Time spent only on relevant awards
  • More precise reviews
